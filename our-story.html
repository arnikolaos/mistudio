Our Story


Founded by Nikolaos Arvanitis, Architect, alongside Antonios Chrysovitsanos, Civil Engineer, MI Architects brings together architectural vision, technical expertise, and an integrated approach to design, development, and construction. Rooted in a strong design philosophy, the firm emphasizes architectural quality, technical precision, and the seamless transition from concept to completion.

Our practice encompasses architectural and interior design, real estate development, construction, and turnkey project delivery. Working with an international clientele across a diverse range of projects, from private villas and residences to larger developments, we combine creative exploration, extensive technical knowledge, and advanced visualization technologies to deliver distinctive architectural solutions that balance aesthetics, functionality, and buildability.

At MI Architects, we believe that exceptional architecture extends beyond design. Drawing on comprehensive experience across every stage of the architectural and construction process, we approach each project with close collaboration, careful planning, and a commitment to quality, transforming ambitious ideas into tangible, enduring spaces — from vision to reality.
